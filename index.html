How the CPU calculates

Airene types two numbers in the Calculator. The app turns this into a tiny program in memory. Step through it and watch who does what.

Each instruction goes through the same cycle: fetch it from memory, decode it, execute it.

Discuss with your partner
  • Which part decides what happens next, and which part actually adds?
  • Why do the numbers travel between memory and the CPU?
  • Draw this as a simple diagram in your own way.

Do more cores always help?

Choose a task and change the number of cores. The bars show roughly how long the task takes.

Some work can be split between cores, some cannot. The share that can be split is different for each task.

Discuss with your partner
  • For which tasks do extra cores help a lot? Hardly at all?
  • Look back at Airene's laptop. Was the CPU really her problem?
